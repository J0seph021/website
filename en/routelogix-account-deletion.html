    • Delete my RouteLogix account

    RouteLogix Auto app, developed by G.A. Logix Logiciels inc.

    Last updated: November 17, 2026

    This page explains how to delete your account in the RouteLogix Auto app, developed by G.A. Logix Logiciels inc.

    1. In the app

    Open Menu ☰ › My account › Delete my account, then confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

    2. Without the app

    Email info@galogix.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "RouteLogix account deletion". Your account is deleted within 30 days and we send you a confirmation.

    3. What is deleted

    • Your account, your display name and your email.
    • Your Google, Facebook or email sign-ins, and your sessions on all your devices.
    • Your groups, your saved places and your saved settings.

    4. What remains, without name or email

    • One security log line: the account's random identifier and the date of deletion.
    • If you had the free first month: a non-reversible fingerprint of your sign-in, so that month is only offered once.

    A subscription paid through Google Play is cancelled in Google Play (Subscriptions). Deleting the account does not stop Google Play payments.

    5. Learn more

    See the RouteLogix Auto privacy policy. Questions: info@galogix.com or 819-252-2000.
