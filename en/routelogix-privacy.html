    The information the app collects and how it is used

    Last updated: November 17, 2026

    This policy applies to the RouteLogix Auto app (Android phone and Android Auto) and to the server that runs it. It explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it and how long it is kept, in accordance with Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25). The general policy for www.galogix.ca is available here.

    RouteLogix Auto does not require an account. The app identifies the vehicle by the operator name you enter and by a device identifier.

    Background location

    While tracking is on, RouteLogix keeps receiving your location even when the screen is off or you use another app. A permanent notification shows it, and you can stop tracking at any time from that notification or from the app. This lets other vehicles see you and lets you send an SOS with the phone locked.

    Crash detection

    Crash detection uses the phone's motion sensors. They are analyzed on the device only. Nothing is sent except the alert itself, if an impact is detected and you do not cancel it. RouteLogix does not guarantee that every crash is detected and does not replace 911.

    What RouteLogix does not do

    • No advertising and no sale of information.
    • No third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app.
    • No access to your contacts, photos or personal messages.

    3. Third-party services

    • Google Play services: location computed by the phone, and speech recognition when you use a voice command.
    • OpenRouteService (HeiGIT, Germany): receives the start point and destination when a route is computed online.
    • OpenFreeMap and the mapping services of the governments of Canada and Quebec: provide map tiles. Like any website, they see the IP address and the map area requested.

    4. Hosting

    The RouteLogix server is hosted by OVHcloud in Beauharnois, Quebec. Communication between the app and the server is encrypted.

    5. Retention

    • Trip history: 90 days.
    • Network quality events: 90 days.
    • Message delivery receipts: 30 days.
    • Radio voice: not kept.
    • SOS: kept in your group's safety history.
    • App settings (operator name, preferences, offline maps): on your phone, until you uninstall the app.

    6. Your rights

    Under Law 25, you may request access to your information, its correction or its deletion, and withdraw your consent by stopping tracking or uninstalling the app. For any request, including deletion of a vehicle's data, email info@galogix.com with the operator name and the device name. We reply within 30 days. You may also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

    7. Changes

    This policy may change. The update date appears at the top of the page.
